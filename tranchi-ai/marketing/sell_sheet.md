2. Agree price + package.
3. Get paid **first** (Zelle / PayPal G&S / Venmo / wire).
4. Deliver the full CSV (Google Drive / email).
5. Offer a **monthly refresh** subscription — the pipeline refills daily at $0

## ⚖️ Keep it clean
- These are **public-record property lists** — legal to sell (standard list
  brokering). You're not selling SSNs or private consumer data.
- If you sell at real scale, glance at **data-broker registration** rules in
  CA/TX/OR/VT (a quick check, not a blocker for property lists).
- Don't promise phone numbers you don't have; sell it as a **mail/skip-trace**
  list and it's honest.
