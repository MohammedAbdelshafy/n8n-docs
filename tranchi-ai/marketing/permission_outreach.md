# Permission-based outreach kit (call / Google Meet / WhatsApp)

The smart, compliant play: don't hard-pitch — **ask permission** to talk, and
let them pick the channel. Same message, three formats below.

> ⚠️ Reality: our seller leads have **mailing address only** — no email, no phone.
> So first contact = **MAIL**. Text/WhatsApp/email only *after* you skip-trace a
> number, or once they reply. And a first unsolicited **text/call** to a number
> you skip-traced is **TCPA-regulated** — mail first is the safe opener; once they
> respond you can call/Meet/WhatsApp freely.

Set up once (free): a **Google Meet booking link** — Google Calendar → Appointment
schedule (or Calendly free) → paste the link where it says `[MEET LINK]`.

---

## 1) MAIL letter — the first touch (this is the one you can send now)

{{owner_name}}
{{mailing_address}}

Hi {{owner_name}},

My name is **[YOUR NAME]** and I buy homes here in the Miami area. I'm reaching
out about your property at **{{property_address}}**.

I'd like to make you a **fair cash offer** — as-is, no repairs, no fees, close on
your timeline. Before I do, I'd just like your **permission to talk** for a few
minutes, whichever way is easiest for you:

  📞 Call or text me: **[YOUR PHONE]**
  💬 WhatsApp: **[YOUR WHATSAPP NUMBER]**
  🎥 Or book a quick video call: **[MEET LINK]**

No pressure and no obligation. If you'd rather I not contact you again, just let
me know and I'll remove your address.

Thanks for your time,
**[YOUR NAME] — [YOUR COMPANY]**

---

## 2) SMS / WhatsApp — first text (only after skip-trace; keep it soft)

> Hi {{owner_name}}, this is [YOUR NAME] — I buy houses in Miami. I'm interested
> in your property at {{property_address}} and can pay cash, as-is. Is it OK if I
> ask you a couple quick questions, or would a short call/video chat be easier?
> No pressure either way. (Reply STOP to opt out.)

*Keep the very first message question-based and non-marketing — that's what keeps
it compliant. Add "Reply STOP to opt out" every time.*

---

## 3) EMAIL — only for contacts that HAVE an email (buyers, or opted-in sellers)

**Subject:** Quick question about {{property_address}}

Hi {{owner_name}},

I'm [YOUR NAME] — I buy homes in the Miami area and I'm interested in your
property at **{{property_address}}**. I can pay cash, as-is, and close on your
schedule.

Would you be open to a quick chat? Whatever's easiest for you:
- Call/text: **[YOUR PHONE]**
- WhatsApp: **[YOUR WHATSAPP]**
- 15-min video call: **[MEET LINK]**

No obligation at all — just want to see if a fair offer makes sense for you.

Thanks,
**[YOUR NAME] — [YOUR COMPANY] — [YOUR PHONE]**

---

## How to run it
1. **Mail** version → your top-10 `hot_deals_call_list` (you can send today).
2. Skip-trace a number → send the **SMS/WhatsApp** opener.
3. They reply → call / Google Meet / WhatsApp freely, run your numbers, get it
   under contract (`closing_kit.md`), assign to a buyer.
