# Where to actually sell the packs (real channels)

**Not PropStream / Reonomy / Privy** — those are data *suppliers* (you pay them
to pull leads). You can't list leads for sale there. Sell where buyers actually
buy:

## 1. Your built-in buyer list (start here — free, warm)
The offers run now writes **`buyer_prospects.csv`** — every owner holding **3+
distressed Miami properties**, with their mailing address. These are **active
cash buyers/landlords**. Mail or contact them: *"I've got a list of 6,557
off-market distressed Miami properties with owner + mailing info — want it?"*
They buy leads AND they buy houses (so they're also who you assign contracts to).

## 2. Lead marketplaces (they broker buyer↔seller lead sales)
- **RealEstateBees.com** — pay-per-lead marketplace for investor/wholesaler leads
- **iSpeedToLead.com** — motivated-seller lead marketplace (sellers list leads)
- **MotivatedSellers.com** — off-market distressed seller leads
- **The Share Group (theshare.group)** — list/data marketing
Contact each: ask if you can **supply** leads (become a lead seller/affiliate),
not just buy. Terms vary; some take a cut.

## 3. Investor communities (post the sell sheet + teaser — free)
- **BiggerPockets** — forums + marketplace (huge investor base)
- **Facebook groups** — search & join: "Wholesale Real Estate", "Miami Real
  Estate Investors", "South Florida Wholesalers", "We Buy Houses Miami",
  "[Broward/Dade] REI". Post the sell sheet + masked teaser.
- **Reddit** — r/realestateinvesting, r/wholesaling
- **LinkedIn** — real-estate investor groups; search "cash buyer Miami"
- **Local REIA meetups** — Florida has many (Dade/Broward REIA); sell in person
- **Craigslist** (Miami → services / real estate) — "Distressed property list"

## 4. Direct to cash buyers (find them free)
- Google: **"we buy houses Miami"**, **"sell my house fast Miami"** — every ad
  is a cash buyer who needs seller leads. Email/DM them the sell sheet.
- Bandit-sign numbers, "cash for homes" billboards, wholesaler websites.
- Hard-money lenders & title companies — they know every active investor.

## The pitch (short)
> "I run a system that pulls fresh off-market distressed Miami properties from
> public records — 6,557 right now with owner name, mailing address, and value.
> Mail-ready. I sell the list or a monthly refresh. Want a free 50-row sample?"

Attach `teaser_sample.csv`. Get paid first. Deliver the full CSV. Offer monthly.

## Reality check on channels
- No API auto-posts your leads to these — you (or a VA) post/DM manually. That's
  the job; it's free and it's where deals close.
- Facebook/marketplaces block bots, so this can't be automated — do it by hand
  or hire a $5/hr VA to blast the sell sheet.
