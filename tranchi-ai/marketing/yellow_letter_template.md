# Cash-Offer "Yellow Letter" — mail-merge template

Merge this against `offer_package_ALL_<date>.csv` (or the top-200 file). The
`{{...}}` fields map to CSV columns. Keep it short, plain, and honest — the goal
is a phone call, not a signed contract in the mail.

**Merge fields available in the CSV:** `owner_name`, `property_address`,
`city`, `state`, `zip`, `mailing_address`, `motivation`, `reason`.

---

## Letter body

{{owner_name}}
{{mailing_address}}

Hi {{owner_name}},

My name is **[YOUR NAME]** and I buy houses here in the Miami area. I'm
reaching out about the property you own at **{{property_address}}**.

I'd like to make you a **fair cash offer** for it — as-is, no repairs, no agent
commissions, no fees, and I can close on the date that works for you. If it's
sitting empty, behind on anything, or just more hassle than it's worth, I can
take it off your hands quickly.

There's no obligation and no pressure. If you'd even consider selling, call or
text me and I'll give you a real number.

**📞 [YOUR PHONE]**
**[YOUR NAME] — [YOUR COMPANY]**

*If you'd prefer not to hear from me again, just let me know and I'll remove your
address from my list.*

## Before you mail — fill these in
- `[YOUR NAME]`, `[YOUR COMPANY]`, `[YOUR PHONE]` — your real contact info (the
  phone must be one you'll answer; this letter's whole job is to make it ring).
- Return address = **you** (the mail house prints it).

## How to actually mail it
1. Pick your mail house / print-and-mail service (yellow-letter vendors used by
   wholesalers, ~$0.50–0.80 per piece all-in).
2. Upload the CSV + this letter; map the merge fields above.
3. **Mail in waves, not all at once.** Reasons that are about *your* money:
   - Every letter that lands can make your phone ring — mail only as many as you
     can personally answer and follow up on in a week.
   - Start with the **HOT (vacant)** rows — highest intent, best response.
   - A 200–500 test wave tells you your response rate before you spend on all
     6,557 (~$3–5k at full volume).
4. When a call comes in: talk price, run your numbers (offer ~25% under value /
   your max allowable), get it under contract, then assign to a cash buyer.

## Compliance notes (keep it clean)
- It's a genuine offer to buy — no fake urgency, no pretending to be an agency.
- Honor opt-outs; drop anyone who asks off future mailings.
- You're contacting property owners by mail about their own property — standard
  for real-estate direct mail. (Cold *calling/texting* scraped numbers is a
  different, TCPA-regulated animal — don't, without consent.)
