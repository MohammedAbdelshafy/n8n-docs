# Closing Kit — from a "yes" to a signed, sellable contract

This is the paperwork + playbook to turn an interested seller into a signed
contract you can **assign to a cash buyer for a fee**. Everything before this
(finding the house, owner, mailing address, the letter) is already done.

> ⚠️ **Not legal advice.** These are generic starting templates. Before you use
> them, have a **Florida real-estate attorney or a title/escrow company** review
> them, and confirm Florida's wholesaling/assignment **disclosure** rules. Most
> title companies will hand you their own vetted contract for free — use theirs
> if offered.

---

## The path to a signed contract (how the money actually happens)

1. **Letter lands → owner calls you.** (No phone needed on your end — they call.)
2. **You talk.** Confirm they own it, it's the distressed one, and they'd sell.
   Ask what they'd want for it. Listen more than you talk.
3. **You run your number.** Max you pay = (what a cash buyer will pay) − (your
   assignment fee) − (rough repairs). Aim to get it **~25% under market** or
   better — that spread is your profit + your buyer's margin.
4. **You get it under contract** — the Purchase & Sale Agreement below, signed by
   the seller, with **"and/or assigns"** after your name (that's what lets you
   sell the contract).
5. **You assign the contract to a cash buyer** (from your buyer list) using the
   Assignment Agreement below, for an assignment fee.
6. **Title company / attorney closes it.** Buyer's money funds; your fee is paid
   at closing. You never buy the house yourself.

## TEMPLATE 1 — Purchase & Sale Agreement (Seller → You)

```
REAL ESTATE PURCHASE AND SALE AGREEMENT

Date: ____________

SELLER: ______________________________ ("Seller")
BUYER:  [YOUR NAME] and/or assigns ("Buyer")

PROPERTY: {{property_address}}, {{city}}, FL {{zip}}
Parcel/Folio #: ______________________ (optional)

1. PURCHASE PRICE: $____________, all cash.
2. EARNEST MONEY: $________ deposited with __________________ (title/escrow)
   within ____ business days of the Effective Date, credited to Buyer at closing.
3. AS-IS: Property sold strictly as-is, where-is. Seller makes no repairs.
4. INSPECTION / DUE DILIGENCE: Buyer may inspect and may cancel for any reason
   within ____ days of the Effective Date, with full refund of earnest money.
5. CLOSING: On or before ____________, at a title company/attorney of Buyer's
   choice. Seller delivers marketable title by warranty deed.
6. ASSIGNMENT: Buyer may assign this Agreement to any third party. Upon
   assignment, the assignee assumes Buyer's obligations.
7. CLOSING COSTS: ____________________________________________.
8. DEFAULT: If Buyer defaults, Seller's sole remedy is the earnest money. If
   Seller defaults, Buyer may seek specific performance or refund.
9. ENTIRE AGREEMENT: This is the entire agreement; changes must be in writing.

SELLER: _______________________  Date: ________
BUYER:  _______________________  Date: ________
```

## TEMPLATE 2 — Assignment of Contract (You → Cash Buyer)

```
ASSIGNMENT OF PURCHASE AND SALE AGREEMENT

Date: ____________

ASSIGNOR: [YOUR NAME] ("Assignor")
ASSIGNEE: ______________________________ ("Assignee")

Property: {{property_address}}, {{city}}, FL {{zip}}
Original Agreement dated: ____________ between Assignor (as Buyer) and
______________________________ (Seller).

1. Assignor assigns all rights and obligations in the Original Agreement to
   Assignee.
2. ASSIGNMENT FEE: Assignee pays Assignor $____________, payable at closing
   (or $______ non-refundable now, balance at closing).
3. Assignee has reviewed the Original Agreement and accepts it as-is.
4. Closing to occur under the terms of the Original Agreement.

ASSIGNOR: _______________________  Date: ________
ASSIGNEE: _______________________  Date: ________
```

## Free skip-trace — the honest version

You already have the expensive part **free** (owner name + mailing address, from
the county parcel match). For a *phone* on a specific hot lead:

- **TruePeopleSearch.com**, **FastPeopleSearch.com**, **ThatsThem.com** — free to
  a human. Search the owner name + city, or the mailing address. ~1 min each.
- These are **bot-protected** — no automating them at scale for free (same wall
  as the other blocked sites). Use them **manually** on your hottest handful.
- Everyone else: reach by **mail** — which needs no phone.

Rule of thumb: **mail all 6,557; hand-lookup phones only for the vacant/HOT rows
you most want to call.**

---

## What NOT to do (keeps you legal)
- Don't cold-call/text numbers you skip-traced without consent — that's
  TCPA-regulated. Mail is fine; unsolicited auto-calls/texts are not.
- Don't present yourself as the government, a bank, or an agency.
- Honor every opt-out.
- Get the contracts blessed by a FL attorney/title company before you sign one.
